pyannote speaker-diarization-community-1
========================================

This distribution includes the voice-segmentation neural-network weights from
the pyannote speaker-diarization-community-1 model, converted from PyTorch to
ONNX for local CPU inference.

Creator: pyannote
Source: https://huggingface.co/pyannote/speaker-diarization-community-1
Bundled revision: 3533c8cf8e369892e6b79ff1bf80f7b0286a54ee
Changes to model files: the segmentation weights were exported to ONNX. Speaker
embedding, PLDA, clustering, and diarization assets are not distributed.
