The segmentation weights were exported from PyTorch to ONNX. Omni Describer
uses only voice activity and does not perform speaker identification,
embedding, clustering, or diarization. Inference is fully local and requires
no account, token, telemetry, or first-run model download.

Omni Describer is not affiliated with or endorsed by pyannote.
